link.nomadamas.org uses Google Analytics 4 to see which channels bring visitors and which links they open, and Microsoft Clarity to see where visitors look and click on the page. This notice page is not measured.

What we collect

  • Pages viewed, language, and time on page
  • Links you open (which channel you left for)
  • How you arrived: the previous page address and values such as UTM tags on the address you came in on
  • Browser and device type, screen size, language setting, and country and city estimated from your IP address
  • How you move around the page: scrolling, mouse movement, and where you click or tap. Clarity turns this into click maps (heatmaps) and replays of visits (session recordings).

We don't collect names, email addresses, or accounts. We use this data only to count visits by channel and clicks by link, and to see how the page is used so we can improve it.

How long we keep it

  • Google Analytics: data tied to cookies and visitor identifiers is kept for 14 months. Most standard reports built from aggregated data are not affected by this period.
  • Clarity: visit replays are kept for 30 days, and heatmap and click data for 9 months. Replays we mark for later are also deleted after 9 months.

Cookies and browser storage

  • _ga, _ga_QG2DERJBRQ: Google Analytics cookies that tell visitors and visits apart. They last up to two years after your last visit.
  • _clck, _clsk: Clarity cookies that group pages into the same visitor and the same visit. _clck lasts up to one year and _clsk one day.
  • MUID, CLID, and others: cookies Microsoft sets on its own domains (clarity.ms, bing.com) when Clarity loads. MUID identifies browsers visiting Microsoft sites, is used for advertising and site analytics, and lasts up to 390 days. CLID records when Clarity first saw this browser on any site that uses Clarity and lasts up to one year. See the full list in Clarity cookies.
  • nd-lang, nd-theme: remember the language and theme you picked. They stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
  • nd-ref: the first screen briefly keeps the page you came from, and the language page deletes it right after passing it to Google Analytics and Clarity. Clarity receives only the site name. It also disappears when you close the tab.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, Clarity does not use cookies without consent. This site does not ask for consent, so Clarity leaves no cookies for visitors from those regions and records each page view separately.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Fonts load from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, so your browser connects to those two services as well.

If you'd rather not be measured

  • Blocking or clearing cookies in your browser settings keeps your visits from being linked together. Blocking third-party cookies also keeps Microsoft's cookies away.
  • With the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on installed, nothing is sent to Google Analytics.
  • Blocking clarity.ms with an ad blocker or your browser's tracking protection keeps anything from being sent to Clarity.
  • You can turn off personalized ads from Microsoft in Microsoft's personalized ad settings.
  • Whichever you choose, every link still opens.
